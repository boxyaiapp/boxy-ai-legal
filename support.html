Support & contact

Questions about Boxy AI, an account, privacy or subscriptions? Use the contact details below.

Subscriptions

For cancellation of a Google Play or Apple subscription, use the management controls of the store that billed you. Deleting the app or your Boxy AI account does not automatically cancel billing.
