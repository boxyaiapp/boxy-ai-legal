PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the main ways Boxy AI handles information when you use the app or contact our support team.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who operates Boxy AI?

Boxy AI is developed by Ahmad Zubedat. For privacy questions, contact support@boxyaiapp.com.

2. Information involved in using the service

  • Account information: identifiers used to sign in, such as an email address and authentication-provider identifiers.
  • Inventory information: box and location names, item descriptions, quantities, QR associations, and other content you choose to save.
  • Images: photos you submit for AI-assisted recognition and any optional photos you explicitly choose to keep with your items or boxes.
  • Support information: feedback, support communications and optional screenshots you submit.
  • Purchase information: subscription and entitlement status, when paid plans are available. Payments are handled through the relevant app store or payment provider; Boxy AI does not need your full payment-card number to provide an app-store subscription.
  • Technical and security information: limited request, diagnostic and security records needed to provide and protect the service.

3. Why we use this information

We use the information to authenticate accounts, save and retrieve inventory, provide optional AI features, manage access to paid features, respond to support inquiries, maintain security and investigate service problems.

4. AI photos and optional stored images

Photos submitted for AI-assisted recognition are sent for processing through our service infrastructure and an AI provider (such as Google's Gemini). Recognition photos are not automatically saved as permanent inventory photos. Temporary processing or retry-related results may be retained for a limited period, including up to approximately 24 hours in applicable workflows. Service providers may process submitted data under their own applicable terms. Photos a user intentionally saves as item or box images are different and may remain until removed.

5. Feedback and screenshots

If you send feedback with a screenshot, that screenshot can be stored privately for support review. A feedback screenshot is not treated as a temporary AI-scan photo. Do not include sensitive information in screenshots unless necessary to explain your request.

6. Service providers

Depending on the feature, Boxy AI uses infrastructure and service providers for authentication and data storage (including Supabase), AI processing (including Google Gemini), sign-in providers (such as Google and Apple), and app-store purchases or subscription entitlements (including RevenueCat when enabled). These services may process information on our behalf or under their own terms.

7. Storage and retention

Inventory and optional saved content generally remain associated with your account while needed to provide the service. Account deletion is intended to remove associated account and inventory data, subject to verification and any legitimate legal, security or financial record-retention obligations. Some temporary technical data expires earlier; optional feedback attachments may have a different retention period from AI processing photos. We do not promise that third-party provider data is deleted instantly when an in-app request is accepted.

8. Account deletion and your choices

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app using our Account Deletion page. You can also ask questions about your account information, corrections or privacy by contacting support@boxyaiapp.com. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an app-store subscription.

9. Security

We use access controls and safeguards designed to protect account information and restrict private files. No service can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, one-time authentication codes or private deletion receipt secrets to support.

10. Updates

We may update this policy as Boxy AI's features and data practices evolve. The updated version and date will be posted here.
