ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete your Boxy AI account

You can request deletion even if you no longer have Boxy AI installed or cannot use its Settings screen.

Request deletion by email

Send a request to support@boxyaiapp.com. Use the subject Boxy AI account deletion request. If possible, send it from the email address associated with your account, or explain if you can no longer access that address.

Email a deletion request

You do not need to reinstall the app to initiate this request. We may need to verify that you own the account before deleting it, to protect you against unauthorized deletion. We will explain the verification and next steps when reviewing your request.

Never email your password, one-time sign-in or MFA codes, recovery codes, or private deletion receipt secret. Do not attach identification documents unless a secure verification process explicitly requires them.

Request through the app

If your version of Boxy AI offers Settings → Delete Account, you can also initiate a request there. If it is unavailable or you cannot sign in, use the email method above instead.

What will be deleted?

When a verified account deletion is completed, associated account data and user inventory (including boxes, items and relevant uploaded assets) are removed as applicable. Some processing, cleanup or third-party operations may take additional time. Limited records may be kept when genuinely needed for legal compliance, security or financial recordkeeping; see our Privacy Policy.

What about an active subscription?

Account deletion does not cancel your subscription or stop future app-store billing by itself. If subscribed, separately manage or cancel it through the store where you purchased it:

  • Manage Google Play subscriptions
  • Apple subscription cancellation instructions

Need help?

For problems submitting a deletion request or identifying the account, email support@boxyaiapp.com. Explain what happened, but do not include private credentials or receipt secrets.
